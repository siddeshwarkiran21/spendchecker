Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address, name, and the financial transactions you choose to log. We may also collect basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type for security and performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the Spendly service. This includes calculating your spending summaries, categorizing expenses, and managing your user account. We do not sell your financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to prevent unauthorized access. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

We do not share your personal financial data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (like hosting services) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about your privacy or wish to request data deletion, please reach out to our privacy officer at privacy@spendly.app

Your privacy is our priority. Thank you for trusting Spendly.
